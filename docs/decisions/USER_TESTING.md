# Wikiplotter User Testing 

## Tasks 

| Task | Instruction | Rationale | 
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Begin exploring a hub. | Find the Avatar: The Last Airbender wiki, set your progress to Book 1, Episode 1, and explore the articles available to you on the first episode. | Evaluate whether hub discovery, onboarding, and browsing are intuitive without guidance. | 
| 2. Update your reading progress. | Pretend you've binged until Book 1, Episode 10. Update your progress and find out information about a character that wasn't there before. | Evaluate how intuitive it is to set reading progress and evaluate user's understanding of its purpose. |
| 3. Register for an account. | Create an account and verify that your reading progress is saved from browsing anonymously. | Evaluate how smooth the account onboarding process is and test progress synchronization. |
| 4. Edit an article. | Visit the "User Testing" hub, find the "Test Four" article, and edit it to update the quoted text to a new kaomoji. Verify that the changes are saved after editing. | Test the ease of the article editing experience and evaluate users' understanding of the article view. | 
| 5. Start your own hub. | Go back to the homepage, and start a new hub with your a name of choice. Create up to ten checkpoints with their own names. Create an empty first article with your title of choice, then upload three images with captions and a sentence block explaining each image. Set appropriate checkpoints. | Evaluate the hub management experience and evaluate users' understanding of the application's features. | 
| 6. Manage your data. | Find the settings page, and update your username. Find your uploaded media in the application, and delete one of your images. Upload a new one directly from the Uploads page. Finally, log out of Wikiplotter. | Test the ease of navigation for settings and data, and evaluate users' understanding of their data in the application. |
